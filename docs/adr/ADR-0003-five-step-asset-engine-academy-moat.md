# ADR-0003: Five-Step Asset Engine Academy Moat Protocol

## Context
Online educational platforms and curricula often suffer from commoditization. A massive catalog of tracks or modules alone does not create an economic moat when competing against established platforms (Reforge, Product School, Lenny, Farnam Street) and automated generative AI content mills. Generating standalone curriculum tracks without intellectual depth or empirical grounding results in generic, low-value courseware.

## Decision
We enforce the closed-loop Sovereign 5-Step Asset Engine Pipeline across all curriculum, research, and platform modules:
Research (Empirical telemetry & studies) -> Concept (Atomic principles & proofs) -> Framework (Operating blueprints & models) -> Diagnostic (Proving grounds & calculators) -> Implementation (Production code & advisory).
The Academy is strictly the operational consumption layer of our primary research and proving grounds, never an isolated content factory.

## Status
Accepted

## Consequences
Positive:
- Every educational module is anchored in verifiable, empirical research and interactive tools rather than theoretical regurgitation.
- Creates an uncopyable platform moat where the software tooling, diagnostics, and concepts reinforce one another in a compounding flywheel.
- Elevates perceived value, justifying executive-tier pricing and enterprise advisory engagements.

Negative/Considerations:
- Higher production threshold: Creating new curriculum modules requires complete 5-step asset backing rather than quick text generation.
- Increased maintenance overhead to keep research citations, interactive calculators, and framework schemas synchronized.
