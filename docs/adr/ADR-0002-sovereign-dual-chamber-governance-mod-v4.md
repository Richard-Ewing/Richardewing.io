# ADR-0002: Sovereign Dual-Chamber Governance (Board & War Room)

## Context
AI-assisted coding environments and multi-agent development often suffer from two failure modes:
1. Superficial vibe-coding and hallucination debt where agents produce unverified, low-taste code and generic corporate platitudes.
2. Committee paralysis where conflicting advice or bloated checklists stall engineering momentum.
We need an architectural governance model that pairs ruthless strategic taste with rapid, deterministic operational execution.

## Decision
We establish the Sovereign Dual-Chamber Governance System in Master Operating Directive (MOD v4.0):
1. **The Board Room (Council of Titans)**: Acts as executive strategic filters examining taste, moats, first-principles velocity, sovereign compute, and constitutional boundaries (Steve Jobs, Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, Mark Zuckerberg, Jensen Huang, Dario Amodei, Sam Altman).
2. **The War Room (General Staff)**: Active operational officers who author, audit, optimize, and clear code/content releases (Paul Graham, Brad Smith, Aravind Srinivas, Brian Balfour, Karri Saarinen, Luis von Ahn, Keith Rabois, Dan Guido, Avinash Kaushik).
3. **Zero-Bloat 3-Tier Clearance Hierarchy**: Approvals are streamlined into Tier 1 (Active Triad on every turn), Tier 2 (Domain Gatekeepers awakened only when their domain is touched), and Tier 3 (Single-Strike Deep Bench on call).

## Consequences
Positive:
- Eliminates AI slop, consulting jargon, and bloated architectural layers.
- Enforces clear separation between strategic invariants (Board Room) and tactical execution (War Room).
- Prevents decision paralysis through the 3-Tier clearance hierarchy, allowing turns to move fast while safeguarding quality.
- Replaces generic prompting with high-signal, domain-specific operational expertise.

Negative/Considerations:
- System complexity: Developers and subagents must maintain context of both chambers and their specific invariants.
- Strict persona adherence requires discipline to ensure subagents adopt genuine human perspectives rather than generic summaries.
