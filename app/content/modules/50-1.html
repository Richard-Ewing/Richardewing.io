Part 1: Lesson 1: The Physics of Module 1: The Fractional CTO Value Prop

To truly leverage Module 1: The Fractional CTO Value Prop, organizations must deconstruct its underlying physics. Core Metrics & Vectors

  • Primary KPI: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) - Direct operational expense attributable to service delivery.
  • Secondary Metric: Gross Margin - Revenue less COGS, indicating core profitability.
  • Risk Vector: Runaway Cloud Spend - Uncontrolled infrastructure expenditure eroding margin.

Part 2: Lesson 2: Economic Teardown & TCO

Every technical decision is inherently a financial decision. Implementing Module 1: The Fractional CTO Value Prop fundamentally alters the balance sheet. By capitalizing the operational overhead and strategically reallocating resources, we extract hidden margin. This teardown meticulously breaks down the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) across critical vectors: compute infrastructure, human capital, and opportunity cost.

Financial Metrics for TCO

  • Direct CapEx/OpEx: Capital expenditures and operational expenses directly tied to infrastructure, software, and services.
  • Human Capital Toll: Cost of internal FTEs, recruitment, training, and opportunity cost of diverting existing talent.
  • Opportunity Cost: Foregone revenue or strategic advantage due to delayed initiatives or misallocated resources.
