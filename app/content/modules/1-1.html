Phase 1: The CTO Mandate

As the Chief Technology Officer, you are not merely a technical leader; you are a primary driver of enterprise valuation. In this module, we dissect the exact operational levers required to align engineering output directly with EBITDA and revenue multipliers.

If your RPE is below $250k, your engineering organization is operating as a cost center rather than an innovation engine.
