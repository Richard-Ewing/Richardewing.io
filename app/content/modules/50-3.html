Part 1: Lesson 1: The Physics of Module 3: Boutique Consultancy Margins

Core Metrics:

Part 2: Lesson 2: Economic Teardown & TCO

Every technical decision is financial. Module 3 implementation alters the balance sheet. Capitalize operational overhead to extract hidden margin. This teardown breaks down Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) across compute, human capital, and opportunity cost.

Capitalizing Operational Overhead:

  • Shift from OpEx to CapEx: Convert recurring operational expenditures into capitalized assets where possible, improving profitability and asset base.
  • Amortization Schedules: Develop amortization schedules for new infrastructure, software, and tooling, aligning with projected value realization.
  • Process Standardization: Reduce operational workflow variability to lower costs associated with ad-hoc solutions.

TCO Components:

Direct Capital & Operational Expenditure:

CapEx/OpEx

Hardware, software, cloud compute, network. Distinguish initial investment from running costs.

Human Capital Toll:

Talent Acquisition & Retention

Salaries, benefits, training, recruitment, attrition impact. Specialized Module 3 skills.

Opportunity Cost:

Strategic Forgone Value

Revenue or market share lost due to misallocated resources or delayed market entry.
