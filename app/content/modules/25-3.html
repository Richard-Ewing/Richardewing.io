PART 2: LESSON 2: Economic Teardown & TCO

Every architectural decision in 25.3 Physical AI Simulation is fundamentally a financial decision. Implementing advanced Cost Analysis fundamentally alters the enterprise balance sheet. By meticulously quantizing operational overhead, we expose and extract hidden margin, shifting from a cost center to a profit driver. This section delivers a granular Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) teardown, dissecting expenses across three critical vectors: compute infrastructure, human capital allocation, and the often-overlooked opportunity cost of inaction. A robust TCO model is indispensable for board-level advocacy.

FINANCIAL METRICS & COST VECTORS:

  • Direct CapEx/OpEx: Hardware procurement (GPUs, interconnects), cloud compute subscriptions, software licensing, and energy consumption. Detail both upfront investment and recurring operational expenditure.
  • Human Capital Toll: Cost of specialized talent (AI/ML engineers, computational chemists, MLOps), including recruitment, salaries, benefits, and ongoing training. Quantify the demand for scarce expertise.
  • Opportunity Cost: The quantified value of foregone innovation, delayed market entry, or lost competitive advantage resulting from sub-optimal simulation capabilities or non-adoption. This is the cost of NOT investing.
