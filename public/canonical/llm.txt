AI-POWERED DIAGNOSTIC TOOLS (Interactive Calculators)
================================================================================

PDI Engine (Product Debt Index)
URL: https://richardewing.io/tools/pdi
Purpose: AI-powered backlog forensic audit to quantify capital leakage
Function: Uses GPT-4 to classify Jira/Linear tickets as Maintenance, Retention, or Growth
Input: Backlog items (one per line), team size, average salary
Output: PDI score (0-100), capital waste amount, capacity breakdown pie chart
Benchmark: Score <50 = Critical (insolvent), 50-70 = Warning, >70 = Healthy

EV-SE Engine (Enterprise Value Scenario Engine)
URL: https://richardewing.io/tools/ev-se
Purpose: Valuation modeling for execution risk quantification  
Function: Calculates how execution uncertainty discounts enterprise value
Input: ARR, revenue multiple, execution confidence, risk factors (scope, tech, talent, regulatory)
Output: Wealth Destruction Gap, risk-adjusted valuation, confidence score
Benchmark: Shows difference between "perfect execution" and "real" valuation

AUEB Engine (AI Unit Economics Benchmark)
URL: https://richardewing.io/tools/aueb
Purpose: Calculate AI margin collapse point for LLM-powered products
Function: Model arbitrage analysis and insolvency curve visualization
Input: Price per user, queries per user, cost per query, active users
Output: Gross margin, monthly revenue/cost/profit, insolvency point, model comparison table
Benchmark: Compares GPT-4, GPT-4o, Claude, Llama costs

APER Diagnostic (Algorithmic Product Engineering Ratio)
URL: https://richardewing.io/tools/aper
Purpose: Revenue Per Engineer efficiency calculator
Function: Benchmarks engineering efficiency against industry standards
Input: Annual revenue (ARR), engineer headcount, cost per engineer
Output: APER score ($), efficiency rating, industry comparison chart
Benchmark: <$200K = Critical, $200-400K = Bloated, $400-600K = Healthy, >$600K = Elite
