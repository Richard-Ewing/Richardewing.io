Copy this entire template. Replace the [BRACKETED] sections with your data from Modules 01-03. Then paste the whole thing into Claude or ChatGPT.

```
- Current data state: [SUMMARY OF MODULE 01, SECTION B]

[PASTE YOUR MODULE 01, SECTION C1 TABLE]

TOP PAIN POINTS:
1. [FROM MODULE 01, D1 - BIGGEST TIME WASTER]
2. [FROM MODULE 01, D1 - SECOND BIGGEST]
3. [FROM MODULE 01, D1 - THIRD BIGGEST]

TEAM READINESS:
- Enthusiasm level: [FROM MODULE 01, F1]
- Tech comfort: [FROM MODULE 01, F2]
- Learning capacity: [FROM MODULE 01, F5]

TOP 10 AI OPPORTUNITIES (RANKED BY ROI):
[PASTE YOUR MODULE 03 MATRIX - TOP 10, SORTED BY PRIORITY SCORE]

QUICK WINS IDENTIFIED:
[PASTE YOUR MODULE 02 QUICK WINS TABLE]

CONSTRAINTS:
- Monthly AI budget: [AMOUNT]
- Time available for implementation: [HOURS/WEEK]
- Regulatory requirements: [FROM MODULE 01, E1-E5]
- Risk tolerance: [LOW/MODERATE/HIGH]

=== END BRIEF ===

Based on this brief, generate a comprehensive AI implementation roadmap with:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (3 sentences)
2. TOP 10 ACTIONS ranked by bottom-line impact
3. QUARTERLY MILESTONES (Q1 through Q4)
4. DEPENDENCY CHAIN (what must happen before what)
5. RESOURCE ALLOCATION (budget + time per initiative)
6. SUCCESS METRICS for each action
7. RISK MITIGATION for the top 3 risks
8. 30-DAY KICKSTART (which 3 things to do THIS MONTH)
```

## STEP 2: REVIEW & REFINE THE OUTPUT

After AI generates your roadmap, use these follow-up prompts to refine it:

### Refinement Prompt 1: Reality Check
```
Review the roadmap you just generated and challenge every assumption:
1. Which items might take longer than estimated?
2. Which items have hidden dependencies I haven't considered?
3. What could go wrong with the top 3 initiatives?
4. Am I being too aggressive or too conservative?
```

### Refinement Prompt 2: Budget Validation
```
Based on the roadmap, create a detailed 12-month budget breakdown:
- Monthly tool costs
- One-time setup costs
- Training costs (in time and money)
- Opportunity cost of team time spent implementing
- Total Year 1 investment
- Expected Year 1 return
- Net ROI
```

### Refinement Prompt 3: Stakeholder Version
```
Rewrite this roadmap as a 1-page executive brief suitable for:
- My business partner
- My board of advisors
- My team leads

Each version should emphasize different things:
- Partner: Financial impact and risk
- Board: Strategic positioning and competitive advantage
- Team leads: What changes for their team and why it's good
```
