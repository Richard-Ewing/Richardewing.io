## DECISION FRAMEWORK: THE 5-FILTER TEST

Before adopting ANY AI tool, run it through these 5 filters:

### Filter 1: Problem-Solution Fit
- [ ] Can I clearly describe the problem this tool solves?
- [ ] Am I buying this because I need it, or because it looks cool?
- [ ] Would I pay someone $X/month to do this task manually?
- **If you can't answer YES to all three, STOP. You don't need this tool.**

### Filter 2: Integration Check
- [ ] Does it integrate with my existing tools? (Check API/Zapier/native)
- [ ] Will I need to change my workflow to use it?
- [ ] Can I export my data if I want to leave?

### Filter 3: Total Cost of Ownership
- [ ] Monthly subscription: $_____
- [ ] Setup time (hours × my hourly rate): $_____
- [ ] Training time (hours × hourly rate): $_____
- [ ] Ongoing maintenance time: _____hrs/month
- **Total Year 1 Cost:** $_____

### Filter 4: Vendor Viability
- [ ] Has the company been around for 2+ years?
- [ ] Do they have paying customers (not just free users)?
- [ ] Is their pricing transparent?
- [ ] Do they have a clear data privacy policy?
- [ ] Are there real reviews on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot?

### Filter 5: Exit Strategy
- [ ] Can I cancel anytime without penalty?
- [ ] Can I export all my data?
- [ ] Is there an alternative I could switch to easily?
- [ ] Am I locked into an annual contract? (Avoid for first purchase)

## VENDOR RED FLAG CHECKLIST

🚩 **Immediate red flags — do not buy:**
- [ ] No free trial or money-back guarantee
- [ ] Pricing hidden behind "Contact Sales" (for SMB tools)
- [ ] No clear data privacy/security policy
- [ ] Company is less than 1 year old with no notable customers
- [ ] Requires annual commitment with no monthly option
- [ ] No customer reviews on independent platforms

⚠️ **Yellow flags — proceed with caution:**
- [ ] Pricing significantly lower than competitors (may be subsidized/unsustainable)
- [ ] Heavy VC funding but no profitability path
- [ ] Features that sound too good to be true
- [ ] Lock-in through proprietary formats
- [ ] No API or export functionality

## TRIAL-TO-PURCHASE DECISION FRAMEWORK

### During your free trial, evaluate:

**Week 1:** Does it solve the problem it claims to solve?
**Week 2:** Is the time to use it less than the time it saves?
**Week 3:** Would my team actually use this daily?
**Week 4:** Am I willing to pay for this with my own money?

### Decision Matrix:

| Question | Yes | No |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Does it save me 30+ min/week? | ✅ Continue | ❌ Cancel |
| Is the output quality acceptable? | ✅ Continue | ❌ Cancel |
| Does it integrate with my tools? | ✅ Continue | ⚠️ Workaround? |
| Would I miss it if it was gone? | ✅ Buy | ❌ Cancel |
| Is the ROI positive within 3 months? | ✅ Buy | ⚠️ Negotiate |
