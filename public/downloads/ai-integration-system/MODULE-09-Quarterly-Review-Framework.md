## HOW TO USE THIS MODULE

Every 90 days, run this review. AI moves fast — what worked last quarter may be obsolete. What didn't exist last quarter may be exactly what you need.

**Time to complete:** 2-3 hours every 90 days
**When to use:** End of each quarter
**Output:** Updated AI strategy with clear continue/stop/start decisions
