## Day-by-Day Action Plan for Immediate Implementation

## HOW TO USE THIS MODULE

This is your day-by-day implementation plan. Each day has a specific task, the tool you'll use, the time commitment, and the expected outcome. Follow it exactly.

**Total time commitment:** 30-60 minutes per day
**Prerequisites:** Completed Modules 01-05 (or at minimum, Module 01)
**Output:** Your first AI workflow deployed and producing results

---

## WEEK 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1-7)
### Theme: Set up accounts, understand your tools, run your first AI task

---

### DAY 1: Choose Your Primary AI Tool (30 min)
**Task:** Create an account on Claude (recommended) or ChatGPT
**Steps:**
1. Go to claude.ai (Anthropic) or chat.openai.com
2. Create a free account
3. Upgrade to Pro ($20/month) — this is your most important AI investment
4. Run this test prompt: "I run a [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE]. Give me 5 ways AI could save me time this week."
5. Save the output — this is your first AI interaction

**Tool:** Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus
**Cost:** $20/month
**Outcome:** Working AI account + first AI conversation

---

### DAY 2: Upload Your Business Context (30 min)
**Task:** Give your AI tool context about your business
**Steps:**
1. Open a new conversation in Claude/ChatGPT
2. Paste your completed Module 01 audit (the full thing)
3. Say: "This is my business. I'm going to work with you over the next 30 days to implement AI. Please confirm you understand my business and summarize the top 3 opportunities."
4. Save this as your "Business Context" conversation — you'll return to it

**Tool:** Claude or ChatGPT
**Time:** 30 min
**Outcome:** AI that understands your business

---

### DAY 3: Set Up Your AI Workspace (45 min)
**Task:** Create an organized system for tracking your AI journey
**Steps:**
1. Create a folder: "AI Integration — [Your Company Name]"
2. Create sub-folders: "Prompts", "Outputs", "Results", "Templates"
3. Save your Module 01-06 files in the root folder
4. Create a simple tracking spreadsheet with columns: Date | Task | Tool Used | Time Spent | Result | Notes
5. Log Days 1-3

**Tool:** Google Drive, Notion, or local folders
**Time:** 45 min
**Outcome:** Organized AI workspace

---

### DAY 4: Identify Your First Quick Win (30 min)
**Task:** Pick the ONE task you'll automate first
**Steps:**
1. Review your Module 02 Quick Wins list
2. Pick the task that: takes the most time, is done most often, and has the lowest risk if AI gets it slightly wrong
3. Write down: "My first AI task is: ___________"
4. Ask AI: "Here's a task I do regularly: [DESCRIBE TASK]. Walk me through how to use you to do it. Include the exact prompt I should use each time."

**Tool:** Claude or ChatGPT
**Time:** 30 min
**Outcome:** Your first AI automation selected and prompt created

---

### DAY 5: Run Your First AI Workflow (45 min)
**Task:** Actually use AI for a real business task
**Steps:**
1. Take the prompt from Day 4 and use it on a REAL task (not hypothetical)
2. Compare AI output to what you would have done manually
3. Note: time saved, quality difference, any corrections needed
4. Refine the prompt based on results
5. Log everything in your tracking spreadsheet

**Tool:** Claude or ChatGPT
**Time:** 45 min
**Outcome:** First real AI-assisted task completed

---

### DAY 6: Email AI Setup (30 min)
**Task:** Set up AI for your email workflow
**Steps:**
1. Pick 3 recent emails you had to respond to
2. Paste each into AI with this prompt: "Draft a professional response to this email from a [YOUR ROLE] at [YOUR COMPANY]. Match my tone: [DESCRIBE YOUR USUAL TONE]."
3. Compare AI drafts to what you actually wrote
4. Create a reusable "email response" prompt template
5. Save the template in your Prompts folder

**Tool:** Claude or ChatGPT
**Time:** 30 min
**Outcome:** Email response template that saves 10+ min/day

---

### DAY 7: Week 1 Review (30 min)
**Task:** Review your first week and plan Week 2
**Steps:**
1. Review your tracking spreadsheet
2. Calculate total time invested vs. time saved
3. Rate your confidence level with AI (1-10)
4. List 3 things that worked well
5. List 1 thing you'll improve next week
6. Share your progress with someone (accountability helps)

**Tool:** Your tracking spreadsheet
**Time:** 30 min
**Outcome:** Week 1 assessment + Week 2 plan

---

## WEEK 2: FIRST AI WORKFLOW DEPLOYMENT (Days 8-14)
### Theme: Build a repeatable AI workflow for your highest-value task

---

### DAY 8: Map Your Highest-Value Workflow (45 min)
**Task:** Document the exact steps of your #1 time-consuming process
**Steps:**
1. From Module 02, pick the process with the highest AI applicability score
2. Write down every single step (even the obvious ones)
3. For each step, mark: "AI can do this" / "Human must do this" / "AI drafts, human approves"
4. Paste the process into AI and say: "Help me turn this into an AI-assisted workflow"

---

### DAY 9: Build the Prompt Chain (45 min)
**Task:** Create a series of prompts that handles your workflow end-to-end
**Steps:**
1. Break the workflow into AI-handleable chunks
2. Create a prompt for each chunk
3. Test each prompt with real data
4. Refine until the output is 80%+ usable
5. Save all prompts in your template folder

---

### DAY 10: Test with Real Work (60 min)
**Task:** Run your complete AI workflow on 3 real instances
**Steps:**
1. Process 3 real tasks through your new AI workflow
2. Track: time per task (old way vs. AI way)
3. Track: quality (better/same/worse than manual)
4. Track: corrections needed
5. Refine prompts based on results

---

### DAY 11: Automation Layer (30 min)
**Task:** See if any steps can be fully automated
**Steps:**
1. Sign up for Zapier (free tier) or Make.com
2. Check if your AI workflow has any steps that could be triggered automatically
3. If yes: set up one automation (e.g., "When new email arrives → forward to AI → draft response")
4. If no: that's fine — manual AI-assisted is still faster than manual-only

---

### DAY 12: Document Your New SOP (45 min)
**Task:** Write the new Standard Operating Procedure
**Steps:**
1. Use Prompt 27 from Module 04 to create an AI-enhanced SOP
2. Include: when to use AI, what prompts to use, what to review, where to save outputs
3. Make it simple enough that someone new could follow it

---

### DAY 13: Measure Results (30 min)
**Task:** Calculate the actual impact of your AI workflow
**Steps:**
1. Compare: old process time vs. new process time
2. Calculate: weekly time savings × hourly rate = weekly value
3. Calculate: monthly projection
4. Calculate: annual projection
5. Document: quality improvements or concerns

---

### DAY 14: Week 2 Review (30 min)
**Task:** Assess your AI workflow and decide next steps
**Steps:**
1. Is the workflow working? (Y/N)
2. Time saved this week: ___ hours
3. Value of time saved: $___
4. Confidence level now: ___/10
5. Ready to expand to a second workflow? (Y/N)
