## THE ROI SCORING FORMULA

### Score = (Revenue Impact × Probability of Success) ÷ Implementation Effort

Each factor is scored 1-10:

| Factor | Score | Definition |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **Revenue Impact** | 1-10 | How much will this save/earn? (10 = $50K+/year impact) |
| **Probability of Success** | 1-10 | How likely is this to actually work? (10 = almost certain) |
| **Implementation Effort** | 1-10 | How hard is this to implement? (1 = trivial, 10 = massive effort) |

**Formula:** `(Revenue Impact × Probability) ÷ Effort = Priority Score`

**Example:** Email automation: (7 × 8) ÷ 3 = **18.7** — HIGH priority
**Example:** Custom AI model: (9 × 4) ÷ 9 = **4.0** — LOW priority (high effort, uncertain)

## SCORING GUIDE

### Revenue Impact (1-10)

| Score | Annual Impact | Examples |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| 1-2 | Under $1,000 | Minor convenience improvements |
| 3-4 | $1,000-$5,000 | Small time savings, minor quality improvements |
| 5-6 | $5,000-$20,000 | Significant time savings, reduced hiring needs |
| 7-8 | $20,000-$50,000 | Major process transformation, headcount avoidance |
| 9-10 | $50,000+ | Revenue generation, market expansion, competitive advantage |

### Probability of Success (1-10)

| Score | Likelihood | Characteristics |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1-2 | Very Low | Requires custom AI, unproven technology, complex data |
| 3-4 | Low | Requires significant customization, uncertain outcomes |
| 5-6 | Moderate | Proven use case, requires some setup and training |
| 7-8 | High | Well-established tools, simple implementation, clear ROI |
| 9-10 | Very High | Copy-paste solution, others have done it successfully |

### Implementation Effort (1-10)

| Score | Effort Level | Time | Cost |
|-------|-------------|------|------|
| 1-2 | Trivial | Same day | Free or under $50/mo |
| 3-4 | Low | 1 week | $50-$200/mo |
| 5-6 | Moderate | 2-4 weeks | $200-$1,000/mo + training time |
| 7-8 | High | 1-3 months | $1,000-$5,000/mo + dedicated resources |
| 9-10 | Massive | 3+ months | $5,000+/mo + full team commitment |
