## STEP 2: AI APPLICABILITY SCORING

For each process you listed, score it on these dimensions:

### Scoring Criteria

| Criteria | Score | Definition |
|----------|-------|------------|
| **Repetitiveness** | 1-10 | How repetitive is this task? (10 = highly repetitive, same steps every time) |
| **Data-Driven** | 1-10 | Does this task involve processing, analyzing, or generating text/data? (10 = entirely data/text based) |
| **Error Impact** | 1-10 | How costly are errors in this task? (10 = very costly, AI oversight needed) |
| **Time Consumed** | 1-10 | How much time does this task consume? (10 = major time sink) |
| **AI Suitability** | 1-10 | Based on current AI capabilities, how well could AI handle this? (10 = AI could do 90%+ of this) |

## STEP 3: HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP DECISION FRAMEWORK

For each process, determine the appropriate level of AI involvement:

### AI Involvement Levels

| Level | Description | Examples |
|-------|------------|---------|
| **Level 1: AI Drafts** | AI creates first draft, human reviews and edits | Email responses, reports, proposals |
| **Level 2: AI Assists** | AI provides suggestions, human makes decisions | Research summaries, data analysis, scheduling |
| **Level 3: AI Automates** | AI handles end-to-end, human spot-checks | Data entry, categorization, formatting |
| **Level 4: AI Monitors** | AI runs autonomously, alerts human on exceptions | Chatbots, inbox sorting, social monitoring |
| **Level 5: Full Automation** | AI handles entirely, no human oversight needed | Backup scheduling, file organization, simple notifications |
