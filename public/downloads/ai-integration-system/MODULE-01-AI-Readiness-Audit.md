### Your AI Readiness Score

| Score Range | Rating | What It Means |
|-------------|--------|---------------|
| 105-130 | **🟢 High Readiness** | You're ready to implement immediately. Jump to Module 03 to identify the highest-ROI opportunities. |
| 75-104 | **🟡 Moderate Readiness** | Strong foundation with some gaps. Use Module 02 to map processes, then Module 03 to prioritize. |
| 45-74 | **🟠 Developing** | You have work to do on data and processes first. Focus on Modules 01-02 to build the foundation before rushing to implement. |
| Below 45 | **🔴 Early Stage** | Start with data organization and process documentation. AI can still help — but start with Module 04's "Quick Start" prompts. |

### Gap Analysis

Based on your lowest-scoring sections, here are your priorities:

- **Low A scores (Business Fundamentals):** Clarify your business model before adding AI. AI amplifies what works — if the fundamentals are unclear, AI won't help.
- **Low B scores (Data Maturity):** Data is the fuel for AI. Invest in organizing, cleaning, and centralizing your data first.
- **Low C scores (Tool Inventory):** You may have tool sprawl. Consolidate before adding more. AI should replace or enhance existing tools, not add to the pile.
- **Low D scores (Pain Points):** High pain = high opportunity. Your worst scores here are actually your biggest AI opportunities.
- **Low F scores (Team Readiness):** Change management will be your bottleneck. Budget time for training and communication.
