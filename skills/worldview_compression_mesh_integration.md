# Skill: Worldview Compression Mesh Integration

## When to Use
Use this skill when modifying, extending, or maintaining pages, tools, articles, or curriculum tracks to ensure all digital assets are explicitly bound as manifestations of a single, coherent research program.

## Governance Principles

### 1. The Master Thesis
Every core index page (Framework, Research, About, Start Here) must lead with or feature the master thesis:
> "Organizations are deploying AI faster than they can govern it. My work focuses on the economic, engineering, operational, and security systems required to keep production AI sustainable. Everything on this site exists to help organizations understand, measure, and control production AI."

### 2. Centripetal Convergence on Runtime Governance
The framework must not present Control/Governance as a flat peer. The 5 operational domains (Economics, Product, Engineering, Security, Operations) must converge into **Runtime Governance** (Exogram) as the final implementation layer.

### 3. Chronological Intellectual Evolution
The research timeline must prioritize intellectual phase progression over raw dates:
- **Phase 1: AI & Engineering Economics** (Unit economics, R&D capital, margins - 2024-2025)
- **Phase 2: Product Economics & Debt** (Product Debt Index, feature deprecation - 2025)
- **Phase 3: Operational AI & Cost FinOps** (SLM vs API arbitrage, FinOps - 2025)
- **Phase 4: Agentic AI Security** (Kill switches, vulnerability mapping - 2026)
- **Phase 5: Runtime Governance** (Deterministic rules, verification - 2026)
- **Phase 6: Exogram Platform** (Runtime physical enforcement - 2026+)

### 4. Subtle ID & Provenance Mapping
- Every framework concept must be tagged with a subtle, low-contrast Framework ID (e.g. `[PAIG-ECON-001]`).
- Every concept must explicitly list its **Provenance** (where it appears in CIO, Built In, books, tools, curriculum) and its **Implementation Layer** (referencing Exogram).
