# Skill: Homepage Narrative Compression

## When to Use
When the homepage needs to be audited or rewritten for executive comprehension speed.

## The 15-Second Rule
Enterprise visitors must understand these 5 things in 15 seconds:
1. WHAT BREAKS — operational failure mode
2. WHAT IT COSTS — dollar impact
3. WHY — root cause (governance gap)
4. WHAT FIXES IT — deterministic infrastructure
5. WHY EXOGRAM — inevitability framing

## Core Principles

### Executive Authority Gateway, NOT Product Landing Page
The homepage sells Richard Ewing as the executive thinker who defines the problem space.
Exogram is the infrastructure born from that realization — Layer 3, not Layer 1.

### Correct Hierarchy
1. **Layer 1**: Executive Diagnosis (AI economics, governance, operational realism)
2. **Layer 2**: Frameworks & Diagnostics (PDI, APER, AUEB, methodology)
3. **Layer 3**: Exogram (the enforcement infrastructure)
4. **Layer 4**: Runtime Infrastructure Catalog (Claude/Cursor governance — on subpages, NOT homepage)

### Two Semantic Planes
- **RichardEwing.io** = executive intelligence infrastructure (CTOs, CIOs, boards)
- **Runtime Infrastructure** = operational containment for agentic systems (engineers, infra teams)

Do NOT let Claude/Cursor/retry loops consume the primary identity layer.

### Headline Structure
- **H1**: Static dominant thesis (NOT a word rotator as headline)
- **Below H1**: Constrained rotating pain language (subordinated)
- **3-Step**: Diagnose → Contain → Enforce (containment is the category wedge)

### What to Avoid
- "80% of AI projects fail" (generic Gartner-speak)
- Product landing page drift
- Unbounded cognitive branching
- Multiple parallel intellectual systems
- Claude/Cursor mentions on homepage (save for /compare, /skills, /failures)

## The NarrativeCompression Component
Always placed between Hero and the first proof section.
Delivers: What Breaks → What It Costs → Why → The Fix → The Engine (Exogram)
Uses a scannable grid layout, NOT paragraphs.
