1. **Spreadsheet Models (`.xlsx`)**:
   - **Dynamic Formulas Only**: Never hardcode calculated totals, margins, payback months, or ROI percentages as static numbers. Use live Excel formulas (`SUM`, `IFERROR`, `INDEX/MATCH`, sensitivity multipliers) so a Director of Finance can stress-test their own assumptions immediately.
