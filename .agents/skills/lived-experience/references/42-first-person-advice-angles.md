# 42 First-Person Educational Advice Angles (HWS v2.0 + HWS-AEO v3.0)

Pruned from 54 brainstormed candidates down to **42 non-overlapping first-person educational angles** across 6 families. Every angle must speak in genuine first person (`I`, `I've`, `I'd`, `my`) and teach a concrete formula, parser behavior, operational diagnostic, or disqualifier -- zero thin rants, zero invented stories, zero cookie-cutter symmetry.

## Hard Anti-Slop & Audience Rules
1. **AEO Top + Human Pivot**: Keep the opening 30-to-45-word standalone factual citation block intact on answer, ATS, comparison, and glossary pages, then pivot straight into first-person field advice.
2. **10th Predictive Word Lexical Discipline**: Skip high-probability corporate autocomplete (*seamless, robust, leverage, unlock, delve, foster, game-changer, transformative, navigate the landscape, production-ready, missing layer, missing piece, the bottom line, at the end of the day*). Use plain spoken nouns and verbs (*shelfware, cleanup, babysitting, scrambled, expensive, messy, quiet renewal*).
3. **100% Unique Sibling Openings**: Never repeat a 5-word opening across sibling items (`insiderAdvice`, `myInsiderTake`, `builderFieldNote.whatINoticed`, `founderAdvice.whatBrokeFirst`, or `industryTrends2026`).
4. **Uneven Rhythm & Early Endings**: Mix short one-sentence punches with 3-to-5-sentence explanations. Skip `Moreover`, `Furthermore`, `Ultimately`, and `That said`. Stop on the last concrete fact or check instead of appending a pep-talk moral.
5. **Domain Voice Separation**:
   - `richardewing.io`: Blunt Product Economist talking to named floor roles (`CFO`, `Director of Finance`, `CTO`, `Engineering Manager`, `VP of Operations`, `Customer Support Manager`, `Product Operations Manager`).
   - `careerwin.ai`: Plain-English hiring insider (6th-grade clarity, zero AI hype) talking directly to job seekers (`Registered Nurse`, `Paralegal`, `Software Engineer`, `Logistics Manager`).
   - `exogram.ai`: Candid systems builder talking to power users, founders, and developers hitting memory walls or unsafe tool mutations.

## Family A (1-7): 10th-Thought Diagnostic Openings
1. **Five-file demo vs 50-folder collapse**: Why a tool shines on a 5-file toy repo and breaks across real database schemas and shared folders.
2. **+10% traffic vs 4x API bill shock**: How light user growth multiplies multi-turn tool-use invoices before anyone notices.
3. **Saturday apply vs Sunday 2:00 AM cron rejection**: How Workday and enterprise ATS knockout timers queue automatic overnight declines.
4. **Recommendation reversed at renewal**: Software I used to praise when pricing was flat before per-seat or credit bills crept up.
5. **Junior typing speed vs Friday staff PR pileup**: Why fast code autocomplete pushes four extra hours of debugging onto senior reviewers.
6. **Polite markdown rule vs turn-20 amnesia**: Why static prompt files get crowded out as conversation windows fill up.
7. **Shadow chatbot paste leak on slow tools**: Why customer data lands in free browser tabs whenever approved enterprise tools lag.

## Family B (8-14): Ease of Start & Week-Two Survival ("What I Look At")
8. **Five-minute zero-manual usefulness test**: What works cleanly right out of the box without reading setup wikis.
9. **Day-14 pinned-tab survival check**: Separating tools I open every morning two weeks in from abandoned week-one tabs.
10. **60-second copy-paste config wire-up**: Replacing long SDK migrations with one pasteable MCP block or CSV/JSON import.
11. **Blank chat box cold-start tax**: Measuring the hidden daily minutes lost re-explaining your stack or work history from scratch.
12. **Data export & lock-in check before buying**: Checking whether you can leave in plain JSON/Markdown/PDF before pasting real notes.
13. **Pricing transparency vs gated discovery/trial traps**: Flagging `$2.70` auto-renewing trial traps and enterprise pricing hidden behind forced sales calls.
14. **Two-minute free self-audit before paying anyone**: Quick free check a reader can run with native tools before spending `$1`.

## Family C (15-21): Daily Workflow & Multi-Tool Handoffs ("How I Use It")
15. **Morning multi-model split**: Routing drafting, code refactors, and research across Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Cursor without losing state.
16. **Babysitting & cleanup time audit**: Timing the minutes spent repairing AI mistakes versus writing cleanly the first time.
17. **Zero-friction reads vs 1-click write approval gates**: Letting read queries fly immediately while halting destructive database/API writes.
18. **Six-second hiring manager split-screen scan**: Where eyes land first in Greenhouse, Lever, or iCIMS scorecards.
19. **Section-proximity dated job bullets vs bottom skill dumps**: Proving skills inside recent `MM/YYYY` roles instead of burying keyword lists at page bottom.
20. **Deterministic pre-commit linter over AI self-checks**: Why deterministic scripts catch secrets, broken HTML, and schema drift where prompts forget.
21. **30-day inactive seat & zombie-feature pruning**: Monthly habit of cutting unvisited dashboards and unused seats before billing locks.

24. **Prompt-caching static tool schemas**: Cutting repeated input token charges by caching unchanging tool manifests across turns.
25. **Hard 2-retry & dollar circuit breakers**: Stopping timeout retry loops before one stuck script burns a monthly token allowance.
26. **Maintenance vs feature payroll Insolvency Date**: Measuring when bug cleanup eats `100%` of sprint capacity.
27. **CFO capitalization vs OpEx audit test**: Separating reusable software assets from throwaway prompt experiments before board review.
28. **Scattered `$80+/mo` chat subscriptions vs one shared vault**: Auditing overlapping personal/team AI bills for fragmented context.

## Family E (29-35): Honest Disqualifiers, Tool Lies & Limits

30. **Exact jobs where the competitor genuinely wins**: Naming one-off tasks, Kanban tracking, or fixed notebooks where another tool fits better.
31. **Why 85%+ keyword match scores get zero interviews**: Exposing resume scanners that reward stuffed nouns while hiring managers reject unreadable bullets.
32. **Two-column X/Y coordinate parser collision**: Showing how left sidebars glue onto right-column job titles during left-to-right PDF parsing.
33. **Font ligature & Word header/footer invisibility**: Why design fonts drop `fi`/`fl` letters and top headers erase phone/email fields.
34. **AI buzzword & invented-metric HireRight backfire**: Why padded percentages and robotic adjectives trigger silent declines and background-check mismatches.
35. **Honest scale limits & thin-evidence caveats**: Stating plainly where small-sample telemetry ends and judgment begins.

## Family F (36-42): Concrete Proof, Named Roles & Natural Stops

37. **Turn-by-turn token cost receipt**: Breaking down Turn 1 prompt, Turn 2 raw tool JSON, and Turn 3 re-read multiplication.
38. **Matched inline architecture SVG or real capture**: Pairing every claim with a concrete diagram or real UI capture instead of stock graphics.
39. **Preloaded live calculator bridge**: Sending readers from prose straight into interactive AUEB, PDI, EV-SE, or ROI calculators.
40. **Named floor titles**: Anchoring advice directly to `CFO`, `CTO`, `Director of Finance`, `Engineering Manager`, `VP of Operations`, `Customer Support Manager`, `Product Operations Manager`, `Registered Nurse`, `Paralegal`, or `Logistics Manager`.
41. **Unwarned side effect of the fix**: Calling out what slows down or breaks when you tighten a rule.
