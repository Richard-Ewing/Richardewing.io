# Empirical Research & 30-Day Grounding Protocol

This skill enforces **Step 1 (Research)** of the Sovereign 5-Step Asset Engine:
$$\text{Research} \longrightarrow \text{Concept} \longrightarrow \text{Framework} \longrightarrow \text{Diagnostic} \longrightarrow \text{Implementation}$$

Every curriculum module, diagnostic benchmark, and strategic essay must start with observed reality from the trailing 30 days, not stale training data or recycled consulting assumptions.

---

## 1. The 5-Source Trailing 30-Day Scan

When researching any topic (e.g. AI inference costs, agent reliability, engineering headcount efficiency):

1. **Practitioner Discourse (Reddit & Hacker News)**:
   - Search active subreddits (`r/MachineLearning`, `r/LocalLLaMA`, `r/ExperiencedDevs`) and top Hacker News threads.
   - Look for what broke, what was unexpectedly expensive, what production teams rolled back, and what engineers complain about after hours.
2. **Executive & Industry Telemetry (X & LinkedIn)**:
   - Look at real commentary from engineering leaders, CTOs, and founders.
   - Filter out promotional engagement bait. Focus on shared failure modes, postmortems, and actual infrastructure migrations.
3. **Multimedia & Tech Deep-Dives (YouTube Transcripts & Conference Talks)**:
   - Extract direct observations from engineering talk transcripts and podcasts recorded in the last 30 days.
4. **Code & Repository Signals (GitHub & Open Source Issues)**:
   - Check trending repositories, open issues, and pull request discussions on relevant frameworks.
   - Identify which abstractions developers are struggling with in real codebases.
5. **Prediction Markets & Macro Signals (Polymarket & Industry Reports)**:
   - Check market odds and macro consensus on timelines, model commoditization, and compute availability.

---

## 2. Extraction Invariants (The Lived Evidence Standard)

1. **Concrete Friction Over Theory**: Prioritize numbers that surprised teams: unexpected token bills, latency spikes, context window exhaustion, or failed migrations.
2. **Merge Overlapping Signals**: Do not report five separate quotes that repeat the same obvious trend. Cluster them into one root cause.
3. **The 10th Thought Principle**: Skip the obvious 9 takes anyone would write on day one. Dig into the second-order economic or organizational tension underneath.
4. **Attribution Integrity**: Every statistic, data point, or benchmark must cite its specific empirical source or company context. Never state unsourced numbers.

---

## 3. Pre-Draft Synthesis Gate

Before writing a single paragraph of copy:
- State the core tension: What does the market believe vs. what is actually happening in production?
- Name the operational cost: Who is paying money, wasting engineering cycles, or losing margin?
- Define the architectural fix: How does our concept, framework, or diagnostic solve this failure cleanly?
