# Mission

## Objective
Establish and enforce a deterministic, industrial-grade operational governance framework across the RichardEwing.io ecosystem, transforming probabilistic content generation into compiled operational cognition.

## Active Priority
Scale the Hub-and-Spoke programmatic architecture, maintain a 100% SEO indexing health score via technical remediation, and ensure strict zero-trust runtime policy adherence (SECS) for all digital assets.

## Success Criteria
- 100% GSC indexability with zero 404/Redirect loops.
- All frontend mutations abide by strict high-contrast, enterprise-grade light-themed aesthetics.
- Cognitive operations rely on deterministic rule sets rather than open-ended LLM inference.
- Structural metadata exactly matches the unified ontology.

## Escalation Conditions
- Discovery of probabilistic or "agentic SaaS" terminology (e.g., hallucination, drift).
- Detection of UI contrast accessibility failures in core modules.
- Unhandled routing exceptions creating 404s in GSC telemetry.
