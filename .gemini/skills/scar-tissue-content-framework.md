# Scar Tissue Content Framework — Signal vs. Slop

## Purpose

This skill exists because AI-generated content is the #1 brand destroyer. richardewing.io positions itself as the authority on AI Economics — if the content reads like ChatGPT wrote it, the entire positioning collapses. Every word must carry earned authority.

## THE DEATH TEST — Kill These Immediately

### Dead Words (Auto-Flag & Replace)

| BANNED Word | Why It's Dead | Replace With |
|---|---|---|
| Unlock | AI slop indicator #1 | Use specific verbs: "expose", "find", "calculate", "quantify" |
| Delve | Nobody talks like this | "dig into", "break down", "rip apart" |
| Spearhead | Corporate cosplay | "lead", "build", "run" |
| Tapestry | Meaningless filler | Cut the sentence entirely |
| Unleash | Cringe | "deploy", "ship", "activate" |
| Elevate | Vague corporate speak | "increase by X%", "move from A to B" |
| Testament | Nobody says this in conversation | "proof", "evidence", "the data shows" |
| Synergy | Immediate credibility death | Describe the specific interaction |
| Leverage (as verb) | Overused to meaninglessness | "use", "exploit", "apply" |
| Ecosystem | Almost always filler | Name the specific components |
| Landscape | The AI word for "situation" | "market", "reality", "what's actually happening" |
| Journey | Patronizing | "process", "path", "the work" |
| Robust | Means nothing without specifics | State the specific capability |
| Seamless | Almost always a lie | Describe the actual integration experience |
| Empower | Condescending | State what the person can now DO |
| Holistic | Academic filler | "complete", "full-stack", "end-to-end" |
| Cutting-edge | Cliché | Name the specific technical advantage |
| Game-changer | Meaningless hype | State the specific outcome with numbers |
| Paradigm shift | Academic slop | "the old way is dead because [specific reason]" |
| In conclusion | Essay mode — nobody talks this way | Just make the point |

### Dead Patterns (Structure Kills)

| BANNED Pattern | What It Signals | The Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "5 Ways to..." | AI listicle bait | "Why [Popular Thing] Is Bankrupting Your [Asset]" |
| "In today's rapidly evolving..." | ChatGPT opening line | Start with a provocative claim backed by data |
| "Let's dive in..." | Content creator filler | Just start with the substance |
| "Are you looking to improve..." | Generic lead-gen copy | "Your [specific metric] is broken. Here's the math." |
| "Studies show that..." | Unspecified appeal to authority | "Last month I analyzed [X] — here's what I found" |
| "In conclusion, I hope this helps..." | Essay mode, desperate | State your core belief and stop talking |
| "Thoughts? Comment below!" | Engagement bait | Drop the mic. Walk away. |
| Bulleted list of generic tips | The entire post is disposable | Build a named framework with specific mechanics |

### Dead Tone

- **Toxic Positivity**: "Just keep grinding! Your time is coming!" — Alienates people in real pain. Replace with honest assessment of difficulty + specific tactical path forward.
- **False Certainty**: "This WILL transform your business!" — Nobody believes this. Replace with "This is what worked for [specific scenario]. Your mileage depends on [specific variables]."
- **Corporate Warmth**: "We're excited to announce..." — Nobody cares about your excitement. Replace with what changed for the USER.

## THE ESSB PROTOCOL — 5-Step Human Content Formula

Every piece of content on richardewing.io MUST follow this psychological framework. It bypasses the "marketing filter" in the brain, hooks with tension, and builds massive trust.

### Step 1: The "Call Bullshit" Hook (First 2 Lines)

Target a sacred cow in the niche and tip it over. Make a definitive, polarizing statement.

**WRONG**: "Are you looking to improve your AI ROI? Here are 5 strategies!"
**RIGHT**: "AI Copilot isn't a productivity tool. It's a $58K/engineer/year maintenance liability disguised as a time-saver."

**Applied to richardewing.io**:
- Tools: "Your AI features lose money. This calculator proves it."
- Advisory: "Your R&D budget isn't fueling innovation. It's funding maintenance disguised as progress."
- Blog: "GitHub Copilot went usage-based. Your engineering org is about to get a very expensive wake-up call."

### Step 2: The Scar Tissue Reveal (Lines 3-5)

Prove earned authority. Show proprietary data, specific failures, or backend experience. Bots generalize; humans specify.

**WRONG**: "Studies show that AI tools can impact productivity..."
**RIGHT**: "I know because I've audited 47 engineering orgs in the last 18 months. The pattern is always the same — the CFO asks 'what's the ROI of our AI tools?' and the VP Eng has no answer."

**Applied to richardewing.io**:
- Reference specific audit findings (anonymized)
- Reference specific dollar amounts found in audits
- Reference specific failure patterns observed across multiple clients
- Use "I looked at..." / "I watched..." / "I built..." / "I broke..."

### Step 3: Unpack the Mechanism (The Matrix Reveal - Lines 6-10)

Rip the engine apart. Show the broken gears. Explain the hidden math or flawed incentives behind the system.

**WRONG**: "Firstly, it lacks efficiency. Secondly, it is suboptimal."
**RIGHT**: "Here's what actually happens: Copilot generates 41% of your new code. Your engineers accept it because it saves 10 minutes. But that code carries $58K/year in maintenance cost per engineer because nobody understands what it does. The METR study proved devs are 19% SLOWER with AI — they just FEEL faster because the boring parts are automated. You're paying more for measurably worse output."

### Step 4: The Un-Googlable Framework (The Hard Truth Pivot - Lines 11-14)

Give them the specific, non-obvious fix. Name it. Make it proprietary.

**WRONG**: "Make sure to maintain best practices and stay consistent!"
**RIGHT**: "The actual play is the Product Debt Index. Stop measuring velocity. Start measuring your Technical Insolvency Date — the exact quarter when maintenance consumes 100% of your engineering capacity. When you can hand your CFO that number, the conversation changes."

**Applied to richardewing.io**: ALWAYS name a specific diagnostic tool, framework, or methodology from the site. Every piece of content must link to a concrete next step.

### Step 5: The Philosophical Moat (The Mic Drop - Last Line)

One sentence. Your core belief. No links. No engagement bait. Drop the mic.

**WRONG**: "In conclusion, I hope this helps you on your journey to better AI governance!"
**RIGHT**: "You don't have an AI problem. You have an AI economics problem."

**richardewing.io mic drops** (use these as templates):
- "Profit is the only metric that doesn't lie."
- "If you can't quantify the cost, you can't govern the system."
- "The tools that promise to save you time are the ones burning your runway."
- "Amateurs deploy AI. Professionals govern it."
- "The CFO doesn't care about your prompt engineering. Show them the P&L."

## EVALUATION CHECKLIST

Before publishing ANY content on richardewing.io, run it through this:

- [ ] Does the first sentence make a claim that would piss off at least 20% of LinkedIn?
- [ ] Is there at least ONE specific number, dollar amount, or data point in the first 3 lines?
- [ ] Does it contain ZERO words from the Dead Words list?
- [ ] Does it reveal a mechanism (HOW something is broken), not just state a problem?
- [ ] Does it name a specific richardewing.io tool, framework, or methodology?
- [ ] Does the last sentence stand alone as a belief statement?
- [ ] Would this sound natural if you read it aloud at a bar after 2 drinks?
- [ ] Is there a specific CTA to a diagnostic tool, not a vague "learn more"?

## VOICE GUIDELINES

Richard Ewing's voice is:
- **Direct**: No hedging. "This is broken" not "this could potentially be improved."
- **Data-backed**: Every claim has a number. No hand-waving.
- **Earned**: References specific experience, not generic platitudes.
- **Slightly pissed off**: The frustration of someone who's seen the same mistakes 100 times.
- **Peer-to-peer**: Talking to engineering leaders as equals, not down to them.
- **Anti-hype**: Explicitly calls out AI hype while providing the economic counter-narrative.
